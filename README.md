## The entry

> Mercury: 'The Intern' — it crams a whole year into 88 days and still gets roasted alive every day.

## Why it wins

Scored against the judge's five criteria, in order:

1. **Planet + funny name + why, in one line.** Mercury is named, renamed "The Intern", and the rest of the line explains the rename in the same breath.
2. **Funny on first read, from the idea.** The joke is the situation, not a wordplay on "Mercury": an intern who races through a full year of work in 88 days and still gets roasted alive daily. The laugh lands the moment you picture it.
3. **The why earns the name — and only fits Mercury.** Two traits no other planet shares: an 88-day year (the shortest in the solar system) and a sun-facing side hot enough to roast. Move the line to any other planet and it stops making sense.
4. **Fresh.** The public feed leans on Mercury as a "Mood Swing" and on puns about the other planets. Nobody has cast Mercury as the overworked intern.
5. **Tight.** One line, 98 characters, no throat-clearing — every word sets up or delivers the joke.

The Neptune line ties on points but loses the tie-break: "sideways" is a stray detail, while the Mercury line stacks two Mercury-only traits (88-day year + scorching days) into a single joke. The Uranus line is visual and specific, but "laid-back" is the first pun anyone reaches for with a sideways planet.
